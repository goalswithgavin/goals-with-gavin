FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a website cost?

Packages start at $249. Simple is $249 (up to 3 pages), Standard is $499 (up to 6 pages) and Pro is $899 (up to 10 pages). The final cost depends on what your site needs — a free quote will pin it down.

What's included in each package?

Simple: up to 3 pages, mobile-friendly design, a contact form or click-to-call button, basic SEO setup and Google Analytics. Standard adds up to 6 pages, custom design matched to your logo and colors, an inquiry form, social links and a Google Maps embed, local SEO and two rounds of revisions. Pro adds up to 10 pages, fully custom design, booking/ordering/scheduling link integration, speed optimization, advanced SEO with Google Search Console and 30 days of post-launch support.

Which package should I choose?

If you just need to get online with the basics, Simple is enough. If you want a fuller site that matches your branding, choose Standard. If you need booking or ordering links or a bigger site, go with Pro. Not sure? Request a free quote and I'll recommend one.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. If your current site is outdated, hard to use on a phone, or just doesn't represent your business well anymore, I can rebuild it. Choose "A redesign of my current site" when you request a quote.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every site I build is mobile-first, so it looks great and loads quickly on phones, tablets and computers.

How does the process work?

1) We talk about your business, goals and customers — through this chat or a quick call. 2) I design and build your site, and every page is reviewed and refined by hand before you see it. 3) We launch it.

Do you use AI to build websites?

Yes — I use AI tools to speed up parts of the design and build, which helps keep prices low and turnaround fast. Every page is still reviewed and refined by hand before you see it.

Do you work with businesses outside New Hampshire?

I'm based in New Hampshire and focus on local and small businesses, but I work remotely, so location isn't a barrier.

How long does it take to build a site?

It depends on the package and how quickly we can finalize your content and details. I'll give you a realistic timeline in your quote.

What about my domain and hosting?

Domain and hosting depend on your setup. I'll go over the options and any costs with you in your quote so there are no surprises.

Can I make changes after the site launches?

Standard includes two rounds of revisions and Pro includes 30 days of post-launch support. For updates beyond that, reach out and we can work something out.

Can you help my business show up on Google?

Every package includes basic SEO setup (page titles and descriptions) and Google Analytics. Standard adds local SEO and Pro adds Google Search Console setup. I don't promise specific rankings — SEO takes time.

Is the quote really free?

Yes — quotes are free with no obligation.
