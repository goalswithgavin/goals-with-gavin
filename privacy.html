Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Gavin Reeder (“I,” “me,” or “my”) collects through this website, builtbygavinreeder.com, how I use it, and the choices you have. I’m a freelance web designer based in New Hampshire.

Information I collect

Information you give me

  • Chat assistant. If you use the chat on this site, I collect what you type, which can include your name, business name and description, current website address, the package you’re interested in, your goals and timeline, your email or phone number, and any notes or questions.
  • Project form. If you fill out the project form on the Contact page, I receive the answers you submit. The form is hosted by Tally (tally.so).
  • Email and phone. If you email or call me, I receive your message or number and any details you share.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics. I use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. This information is collected in aggregate and is not used to identify you personally.
  • Fonts. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers and your IP address is shared with Google when a page loads.
  • Chat history in your browser. To keep your conversation going as you move between pages, the chat saves it in your browser’s session storage. It stays on your device and is cleared when you close the tab.

How I use your information

  • To respond to your questions and requests, including preparing quotes and following up about website projects.
  • To provide, improve and maintain the website and the chat assistant.
  • To understand site traffic and which pages and features are useful.

I don’t sell your personal information, and I don’t use what you share in the chat or forms for advertising.

Who I share it with

I share information only with the service providers that help me run the site and communicate with you, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Google (Google Analytics and Google Fonts).
  • Tally (project form).
  • Email and form providers that deliver your chat or form submissions to me, such as Gmail or a form-handling service.

I may also disclose information if required by law or to protect my rights. These providers have their own privacy policies, which govern how they handle your data.

Cookies and tracking choices

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. The site works without cookies, though blocking them may affect analytics.

How long I keep information

I keep messages and project details for as long as needed to respond to you, work together on a project, and keep reasonable business records. You can ask me to delete your information at any time (see “Your choices” below). Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics’ retention settings.

Security

I take reasonable steps to protect the information I receive, but no website or method of electronic storage is completely secure, so I can’t guarantee absolute security. Please don’t share sensitive information, such as passwords, payment card numbers or Social Security numbers, through the chat or forms.

Your choices

You can ask me to tell you what information I have about you, correct it, or delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws. To make a request, email or call me using the contact details below, and I’ll respond within a reasonable time.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for business owners and isn’t directed to children under 13. I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given me information, contact me and I’ll delete it.

Links to other sites

This site links to third-party sites, such as TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and the websites of past clients. I’m not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. When I do, I’ll change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
